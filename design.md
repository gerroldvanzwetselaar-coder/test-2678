# Van Dolderen — Homepage design

Dutch, premium but approachable machinery retail. Refresh the supplied vdolderen.nl identity rather than replacing it. Web only.

## Brand and typography
- Preserve the red/black/grey logo in the white header.
- Crimson #ad1731 CTAs; charcoal #202522; warm white #f7f7f3; sage #e9ede6.
- Poppins display and body, tighter heading tracking, comfortable body leading.
- Large lifestyle photo, dark left-to-right overlay, generous editorial whitespace, crisp rectangular product cards.
- Content width 1240px, desktop gutters 64px, mobile 24px. Section spacing 80–96px.

## Page
Utility bar, logo/search header, main navigation, robot mower hero, assurance strip, category browse, product-family tabs, advice/service story, showroom/contact footer.

## Interactions
Product and shop links open existing webshop. Local search results and mobile navigation. Tabs switch curated products. Phone/email/maps use verified business details. No invented stock or checkout.

## Architecture
Frontend homepage component, composition entry, global styles and public assets only. API, database and analytics unchanged. Product photos and business information from vdolderen.nl. Hero lifestyle photograph from Husqvarna search result.
